## FORMA House
**Buyer-first real estate website concept / UX strategy + front-end engineering**

FORMA House is a self-initiated concept for a residential developer in Lagos. The goal was to explore how a real estate website can do more than present attractive architecture: it can reduce uncertainty, make costs easier to understand and help potential buyers choose a useful next step.

I designed a distinct editorial visual system and built a multi-page responsive experience covering property discovery, property details, payment-plan exploration, buyer education, viewing requests and contact. The prototype includes working listing filters, a payment calculator, an FAQ accordion and validated demo enquiry flows with privacy-conscious confirmation states. Each route has its own content hierarchy and responsive behavior rather than stretching one landing page across every user need.

**The design principle:** clarity before pressure. Property information is presented as sample data, and the concept avoids fabricated trust badges, testimonials or business results.

**Scope:** Information architecture, UX rationale, responsive UI, front-end implementation and interaction prototype.

**Status:** Fictional concept project. Sample brand, homes, prices and content were created for demonstration; enquiry forms do not transmit data, and no real client engagement or live business outcomes are claimed.
